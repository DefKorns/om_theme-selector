# Changelog

## v3.0.0

A ground-up rewrite: the menu is now its own app, with a preview grid, in-app theme downloads, a DIY theme builder, and a theme and music for every folder. Requires [Options Menu](https://github.com/DefKorns/OptionsMenu/releases) v2.1.0 or newer and the Font Remount mod.

- **Theme Manager:** browse installed themes in a preview grid, apply with **A**, hold **B** to delete (the active theme can't be deleted)
- **Download themes:** browse the online catalog for your console and install from it, with a progress bar, free-space check and author credits. **All Themes** downloads the whole catalog (needs a USB/SD drive)
- **Theme Per Folder / Music Per Folder:** give any folder, and Home, its own theme or music. Folders on *Automatic* use their parent's
- **DIY themes:** build a theme from pieces of the ones you already have (Base UI, background, walls, characters, pole, NES background color)
- **Settings:** Theme Randomizer, Audio Randomizer, Clean Up and Reset Settings
- **Every console and region:** NES, Famicom, Famicom Shonen Jump, SNES (USA/EUR) and Super Famicom. Font names and color palette are adjusted to your firmware, with custom fonts per theme
- Nine languages, as in the Options Menu
- Installing it uninstalls Theme Selector 2, Theme Randomizer and Advanced Music Hack, and keeps your themes, music and last applied theme. Every option in **Settings** is turned off again after updating
