## Theme Selector 3.0.0

Theme Selector 3 is a ground-up rewrite. The menu is now its own app, with a preview grid, in-app theme downloads, a DIY theme builder, and a theme and music for every folder.

## Requirements

- [Options Menu v2.1.0](https://github.com/DefKorns/OptionsMenu/releases) or newer. Older Options Menu versions don't work.
- Font Remount mod (`font_remount`), one of hakchi's built-in mods. It applies the theme fonts.
- To download themes from the console: the Hakchi Wi-Fi mod (WPA Supplicant) from KMFDManic's store in hakchi, and a USB Wi-Fi adapter (the TP-Link TL-WN725N is recommended).
- A USB/SD drive is recommended: themes are often 5–30 MB each.

Installing it uninstalls Theme Selector 2, Theme Randomizer and Advanced Music Hack, and keeps your themes, your music and your last applied theme.

## What's new

### Theme Manager
- Browse your installed themes in a preview grid and apply one with **A**. The theme you're using is outlined.
- Hold **B** on a theme to delete it, after a confirmation. The theme you're using can't be deleted.
- Same look as the Options Menu, with its colors taken from `/etc/options_menu/theme.cfg`; the outline of the active theme has its own key, `ActiveThemeBorder`.
- Only themes and options that fit your console are shown.

### Download themes
- Browse the online catalog for your console and install themes straight from it, each one credited to its author. Themes added to the catalog since you last looked come first.
- A progress bar shows each download, with the free space left where the theme is going. A full NAND is refused instead of filled.
- **All Themes** downloads the whole catalog at once and skips the themes you already have. It only shows up with a USB/SD drive connected.
- Downloading a theme you already have asks before overwriting it.
- Theme packages copied by hand into the themes folder are unpacked the next time you open **Themes**.

### Theme and music per folder
- **Theme Per Folder:** give any folder, and Home, its own theme from a folder tree. Folders on *Automatic* use their parent folder's theme. Themes named after a folder, and a theme named `default` for Home, still work.
- **Music Per Folder:** the same, with the `.wav` files in your `music_menu` folder.

### DIY themes
Build your own theme from pieces of the themes you already have: Base UI, background, walls, characters, pole, or the background color on a NES. Every piece is previewed, identical pieces from different themes are listed once, and you can add your own.

### Settings
- **Theme Randomizer:** a different theme each time you change folder.
- **Audio Randomizer:** random Home music, optionally on every folder.
- Switches toggle in place, without reloading the screen.
- **Clean Up** runs on its own after installing, and **Reset Settings** puts the console back to its original theme without uninstalling anything.

### Every console and region
- NES, Famicom, Famicom Shonen Jump, SNES (USA/EUR) and Super Famicom.
- A theme made for one region works on the others: its font names and color palette are adjusted to your firmware.
- Custom fonts per theme.
- Nine languages, as in the Options Menu.

## Updating

Install it over the previous version. Your themes, music, applied theme and folder assignments are kept, but every option in **Settings** is turned off again: turn back on the ones you use.
